# Sales Pipeline Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard tracking sales pipeline health — opportunity count, revenue, and stage-weighted factored revenue — broken down by region, sales stage, opportunity size, and partner involvement. Modeled after Microsoft's official Power BI dashboard example.

## 📊 Overview

This project visualizes a sales opportunity pipeline across:
- **Opportunity Count** – total number of active deals
- **Total Revenue** – sum of all opportunity revenue
- **Factored Revenue** – revenue weighted by how far a deal has progressed through the sales stages
- **Region** – East, Central, West breakdown
- **Sales Stage** – Lead → Qualify → Solution → Proposal → Finalize
- **Opportunity Size** – Small, Medium, Large
- **Partner Driven** – whether a deal involves a partner
- **Monthly Trend** – revenue tracked across Jan–Dec

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop** – data modeling and visualization
- **Excel** – source dataset
